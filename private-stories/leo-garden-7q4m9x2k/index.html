Léo and the Garden Cats
A little hide-and-seek story

Léo and the
Garden Cats

Made for little readers and big imaginations

Page one

A sunny arrival

On a sunny morning, two-year-old Léo went to visit Mamie and Papi in their big house in the French countryside.

Page two

Hello, Minuit!

“Miaou!” There was Minuit, the black cat, sitting by the red roses.

Léo found him right away.

Page three

Where is Nuage?

But where was Nuage, the white cat?

Léo peeped between the tall purple lavender. “Not here!”

Page four

Not under the bench

Léo looked behind the apple trees and under the old wooden bench.

“No Nuage.”

Page five

A tiny clue

Something white slipped past the blue watering can.

“Was that a little tail?”

Page six

Found you!

Two white ears popped up from Mamie’s washing basket.

It was Nuage! Léo clapped his hands. “Found you!”

The end

Together at last

Two cats found.
One very happy day.
